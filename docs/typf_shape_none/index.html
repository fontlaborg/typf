When you just need characters laid out: the simplest possible shaper

This is our “just the basics” shaper. No ligatures, no kerning, no complex script support. Just takes your text, finds the matching glyphs, and lays them out left-to-right. Perfect for ASCII, debugging, or when you don’t want the complexity of HarfBuzz.
